# Sleep Levels

Are you tired all the time? That means, you might be a sleep level 2, or `SL2`. Here I'll define the different sleep levels and their definitions, in case you were wondering. The number after the sleep level indicates your consciousness, ranging from 0-3.

## Awake

In your normal state, a few hours after waking up from a typical good night's sleep (7-8 hours) and without (too much) caffeine, that is a sleep level one, or `SL1`. In this state, you are awake, and your energy level doesn't bother you. Ideally, you want to spend most of the day at this level.

## Asleep

Skipping ahead a bit, being asleep is a sleep level of 3, or `SL3`. This is the highest sleep level. At this level you are voluntarily unconscious.

## Tired

There is a range of being tired as you can experience different intensities of being tired. This will be defined as the range between `SL1` and `SL3`. To create an anchor point, I'll define a sleep level of two (or `SL2`) as the feeling you get in the afternoon after a previous night's sleep of about 4 hours. You might also feel like this after a long exhausting day.

In this range, decimal values are also allowed. A sleep level of `SL2.5` could be experienced after an all-nighter, or also the feeling you get right before falling asleep or right after waking up.

A sleep level of `SL1.5` could be defined as the feeling you have on a lazy Sunday, or a general tiredness from waking up early from your alarm clock. I personally experience this one all the time. It's a frustrating feeling between being too tired and wide awake. You aren't tired enough to snooze, but aren't awake enough to get any chores done.

## Energized

Right after an intense workout, or chugging a caffeinated beverage, your mind might feel extra sharp or wired. This is the highest level of consciousness. This is defined as sleep level zero, or `SL0`. You can think of it as there is "zero" chance of falling asleep anytime soon.

There can also be decimal values between the energized state and awake state. For example, a sleep level of point five, or `SL0.5` is a satisfying state of hyper-focus of the task at hand also known as the "flow state".

## Summary

| Sleep Level | Description | Consiousness |
| ----------- | ----------- | ------------:|
| `SL0.0` | Energized, wired | ++100% |
| `SL0.5` | Flow state | +100% |
| `SL1.0` | Awake (standard) | 100% |
| `SL1.5` | Annoyingly tired | 75% |
| `SL2.0` | Sleep deprived | 50% |
| `SL2.5` | Near sleep | 25% |
| `SL3.0` | Fully asleep | 0% |

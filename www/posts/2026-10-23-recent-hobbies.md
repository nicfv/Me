In fact, my total activity distance for the month of September was a whopping 175km! (And that's only for walks that I measured.) So far for this month, I'm well on track at 89km of running!

## Gaming

I've also been doing a bit of gaming. I recently set up a [Minecraft Server with Docker](./2026-09-20-mc-server-docker.md) on Minecraft version 1.7, released in 2014! Minecraft 1.7 made the base game much more stable, and added a ton of new features such as new blocks, commands, and a ton of new biomes.

The game is still in-progress. Our base is set up in a mega-taiga biome (one of the new additions) and we've reached the Nether, and I've built a decently-producing mob farm. Right now I still need to slay several enderman in order to gain the items needed to complete the end portal, which we haven't looked for yet.

## Gardening

I've made a few updates to my garden, adding a "rock river" which meanders through the middle of the garden. I've uprooted some of the aloe vera plant offshoots, and planted them around the river. I have several empty pots ready to be used for potted plants.

## Reading

I bet you weren't expecting to see this one! Believe it or not, I've recently finished not one but *two* books! The first one was *Tell Me What You Did* by Carter Wilson. In an extremely short summary that doesn't do it any justice, this book is about a podcaster who interviews criminals, asking the title of the book. In the book, someone joins her podcast for an interview, claiming to be her mother's killer, and manages to turn the tables on her. I don't want to spoil too much, but it's a crazy story.

I liked that book so much, that I decided to check out another book by the same author, called *Mister Tender's Girl*. This not only takes place in the same "universe" as *Tell Me What You Did*, but the main characters actually know each other. This book was about a girl who had a tragic accident when she was younger which triggered crazy stalkers to worship her in twisted ways. I highly recommend either of these books if you like mysteries or crime.

Believe it or not, I've actually started a third book, called *The Cuckoo's Egg*, recommended by my colleagues. I've only read the first chapter, but it seems like it will be about a scientist who has to counterstike against a hacker who cracked into the national laboratory network.

# Productive Doomscrolling

Doomscrolling is a major timesuck for thousands of people. But more than that, it's making people's attention spans decrease, which negatively impacts learning, and the ability to read books and write long narratives. The worst part? It's not their fault! These apps are engineered to be maximally addictive. And humans have evolved to have addictive personalities, to survive. For example, when food was scarce, us humans adapted to receive a reward chemical in our brains when we ate extra fatty foods, which are the most calorie-dense.

I don't know what evolutionary advantage we are hanging onto when we doomscroll, but most people can't control it. I know that I can't.
